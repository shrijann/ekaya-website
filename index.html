About The Band

Ekaya Hami is a Nepali folk-fusion music ensemble based in Bhaktapur. Blending traditional Newa and Nepali folk tunes with modern acoustic arrangements, Ekaya brings ancient cultural narratives to life through live sessions and original compositions.
